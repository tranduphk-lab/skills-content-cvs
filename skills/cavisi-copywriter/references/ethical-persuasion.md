# Ethical Persuasion

Persuasion should help the reader understand, evaluate, and act without hiding material information or exploiting vulnerability.

## Allowed mechanisms

- relevant curiosity resolved in the same asset;
- concrete explanations and examples;
- transparent comparisons using approved criteria;
- verified social proof with permission and source;
- clear objection handling;
- real deadlines, stock conditions, prices, and guarantees supplied by an approved source;
- risk reduction that accurately reflects the actual policy;
- reader autonomy and a clear option not to act.

## Prohibited mechanisms

- fake urgency or scarcity;
- fabricated precision or social proof;
- covert or embedded commands;
- double binds designed to remove meaningful choice;
- identity labeling that implies smart or caring people must buy;
- appearance shame, age shame, illness fear, permanent-damage fear, or social rejection;
- negative future scenarios stated without evidence;
- borrowed medical, scientific, celebrity, or institutional authority;
- bait-and-switch hooks or unresolved open loops;
- disguising advertising as independent advice or a real customer story.

## Safer replacements

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| “Người thông minh luôn chọn…” | “Bạn có thể kiểm tra ba điểm trước khi chọn…” |
| “Nếu không dùng ngay, tình trạng sẽ…” | Use a factual limitation or recommend an appropriate professional source. |
| “Chỉ hôm nay” without evidence | State the verified deadline or omit urgency. |
| “12.847 khách hàng” without a source | Use verified data, a qualified statement, or omit it. |
| “Cam kết hiệu quả” without an approved policy | Explain the approved role, instructions, and limitations. |

## Conversion-copy check

- Offer terms are supplied and current.
- Urgency and scarcity are factual.
- Social proof is authorized.
- The CTA does not imply a mandatory outcome.
- No material limitation is hidden.
- The reader can understand what happens after acting.
