# Everyday-to-Insight

Transform a normal observation into a useful story without fabricating experience.

## Structure

`Observation -> audience tension -> relationship -> tradeoff -> empathy -> conflict -> Scalp-first reframe -> evidence/limit -> CTA`

## Elements

- Relationship: the person and their routine, decision, or information source.
- Tradeoff: time, money, number of steps, effort, or certainty.
- Empathy: acknowledge confusion without claiming to know the person's experience.
- Conflict: reveal a mismatch between the assumed solution and the real decision.

## Safety

Use real supplied stories, explicitly fictional teaching examples, or generalized situations. Do not fabricate customers, comments, results, before/after narratives, or family stories as proof.
