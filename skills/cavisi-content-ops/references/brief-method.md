# Brief Method

## Brief sequence

1. Confirm objective, audience, awareness level, and primary tension.
2. Lock one promise and one CTA.
3. Choose hook family and content spine.
4. Separate educational facts, brand language, and product claims.
5. Map sources and review markers.
6. Define format, channel behavior, visual purpose, and repurpose plan.
7. Define owner, approvers, dependencies, and status.
8. Add a test hypothesis when the asset is part of an experiment.

## Production gate

`PRODUCTION-READY` requires all of the following:

- factual statements have suitable sources;
- product wording matches approved records;
- required legal/product/science/publication reviews are complete;
- asset rights and versions are known;
- channel, owner, CTA, and publish state are defined.

If any condition is missing, keep the status at `DRAFT` or `REVIEW`.
