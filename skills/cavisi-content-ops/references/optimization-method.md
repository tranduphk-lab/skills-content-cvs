# Optimization Method

## Diagnose by layer

Check in order:

1. Distribution: did the intended audience receive it?
2. Hook: was the topic and stake clear?
3. Promise: was the expected payoff relevant and proofable?
4. Structure: did the body answer the open loop?
5. Evidence: was the support understandable and credible?
6. Format/channel: was the idea expressed appropriately?
7. CTA: was the next step singular and proportionate?
8. Audience/pillar fit: was the content solving a real tension?

## Decision states

- `KEEP`: meets its objective; continue without major change.
- `REVISE`: direction is valid but one layer underperforms.
- `STOP`: weak strategic fit, unsafe claim, or repeated failure after fair testing.
- `SCALE`: repeat the mechanism across related topics/channels without copying the asset verbatim.
- `TEST`: evidence is insufficient; isolate one variable.

## Test discipline

- Change one major variable when practical.
- Do not strengthen a claim to improve retention.
- Define hypothesis, metric, comparison, duration/sample requirement, and decision rule before the test.
- Keep successful mechanics; do not blindly preserve wording, creator style, or trend packaging.
