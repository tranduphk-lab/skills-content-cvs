# Strategy Method

## Separate the hierarchy

1. Business goal: commercial outcome.
2. Marketing goal: change in market behavior or demand.
3. Content objective: what content must help the audience understand, feel, or do.
4. KPI: observable signal tied to the content objective.

Do not replace missing business goals with vanity metrics.

## Strategy sequence

1. Define planning horizon and constraints.
2. Select one primary audience and document secondary audiences.
3. Map the decision journey: recognize -> understand -> verify -> consider -> act -> maintain.
4. Define 3-5 content objectives.
5. Select pillars and their roles.
6. Define channel roles rather than duplicating all content everywhere.
7. Define measurement by objective.
8. Record assumptions, dependencies, owners, and unresolved approvals.

## Default pillars

Use as a starting hypothesis, not an immutable taxonomy:

- `Hiểu da đầu`: foundational education.
- `Rõ từng bước`: Shampoo/Spray and two-step clarity.
- `Evidence có giới hạn`: source, status, claim literacy.
- `Routine dễ duy trì`: useful decision support.
- `Cavisi world`: philosophy, people, process, community.

## Strategy quality gate

A strategy is usable when the team can explain:

- whom the content serves;
- the audience tension;
- what each pillar changes;
- why each channel exists;
- what will be measured;
- what evidence or operational dependency can block execution.
