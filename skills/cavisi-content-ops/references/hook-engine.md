# Cavisi Hook Engine

A hook must make the topic clear and create a legitimate reason to continue.

## Hook families

### Confusion or decision risk

`Bạn đang [hành động], nhưng đã rõ [vai trò/câu hỏi quyết định] chưa?`

Use for role confusion and routine decisions. Do not claim hidden damage.

### Wasted effort

`Trước khi [mua/thêm/đổi], hãy kiểm tra [information needed].`

Use for time, money, and unnecessary complexity. Do not promise savings or superior outcomes without data.

### Avoid misunderstanding

`[Scientific/product term] chưa tự động chứng minh [finished-product outcome].`

Use for claim and evidence literacy. Do not attack a competitor without verified comparative evidence.

### Solve one difficulty

`Nếu đang phân vân [question], hãy bắt đầu bằng [one check].`

Use for actionable education. Do not invent product directions.

### Better informed future

`Chăm sóc tốt hơn bắt đầu từ việc hiểu đúng [decision].`

Use for brand building. Keep it connected to an immediate audience tension.

## Hook score

Score clarity, specificity, curiosity, stakes, proofability, and Cavisi fit from 1-5. Public-facing hooks require clarity, proofability, and Cavisi fit of at least 4.

## Anti-patterns

- fear without evidence;
- hidden topic;
- borrowed authority;
- universal statements;
- result guarantees;
- unsupported numbers;
- invented personal/customer experience;
- hook stronger than the body can support.
