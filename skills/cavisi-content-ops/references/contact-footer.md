# Cavisi Contact Footer

This footer is mandatory for every public-facing Cavisi content unit unless the user explicitly requests a different footer or a platform constraint makes the full block impossible. It must be appended at the end of captions, social posts, carousel copy, website content blocks, email copy, content series episodes, and repurposed variants.

```text
Cavisi
Hotline/Zalo: 076 435 8668
Website: https://cavisi.vn
Địa chỉ: Toà Leadvisors Tower, 643 Đ. Phạm Văn Đồng, P, Nghĩa Đô, Hà Nội, Việt Nam
```

### Footer rules

- Preserve the phone number, website, address, spelling, and diacritics exactly.
- Keep the footer separate from the editorial body with a divider or blank line.
- Do not turn the footer into a pressure CTA, urgency message, discount claim, or product claim.
- Do not add unapproved operating hours, email, social handles, maps, pricing, or service promises.
- When a platform has a character limit, use this compact form without changing factual values: `Hotline/Zalo: 076 435 8668 | Website: https://cavisi.vn | Địa chỉ: Toà Leadvisors Tower, 643 Đ. Phạm Văn Đồng, P, Nghĩa Đô, Hà Nội, Việt Nam`.
- Do not duplicate the footer when a repurposed content variant already contains it.
- Internal strategy, briefs, test logs, and review documents do not need the public footer unless they include final copy intended for direct publishing.
