# Awareness Ladder

Use the ladder to match content depth to the audience's current question.

1. `Recognize`: notice a daily question or confusion.
2. `Reduce effort`: make the decision easier to approach.
3. `Avoid misunderstanding`: read claims, terms, and sources more critically.
4. `Solve`: answer one concrete routine or product-role question.
5. `Build informed belief`: connect the decision to Cavisi's long-term philosophy.

## Distribution guidance

- Discovery content usually starts at Levels 1-3.
- Website/evidence content can operate at Levels 3-5.
- A campaign can progress from Level 1 to Level 5.
- Level is distance from the immediate need, not content quality.
- Do not force technical microbiome language into low-awareness content.
