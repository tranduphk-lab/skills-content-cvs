# Channel Framework

Channel recommendations are hypotheses until current Cavisi channels and analytics are supplied.

| Channel role | Typical purpose | Suitable outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Website | source of truth, system explanation, evidence | landing page, FAQ, evidence card, product explanation |
| Short video | discovery, problem recognition, concise education | hook-led explainer, FAQ, myth/mechanism |
| Carousel | structured explanation and saving | checklist, comparison, source literacy |
| Facebook/community | discussion and longer context | FAQ, story, community question, recap |
| Email | nurture, routine support, follow-up | education sequence, product guidance after approval |
| Ecommerce | decision support and conversion | approved product facts, role, directions, limitations |

## Repurpose rules

Repurpose the idea, not the exact asset.

For each channel adapt:

- opening context;
- depth;
- visual density;
- source placement;
- CTA;
- interaction expectation.

Never remove a necessary limitation merely because a channel favors shorter content.
