## Claims and products

When a task mentions ingredients, formula, product directions, frequency, efficacy, microbiome effects, clinical testing, dermatologists, safety, treatment, or user outcomes, locate the newest approved product/claim record. If none exists, use the narrowest brand-level language and add the appropriate review marker.

## Reference creators

Reference media can support:

- retention mechanics;
- hook taxonomy;
- story structure;
- topic framing;
- pacing and transition observations.

They cannot establish Cavisi product facts, claims, audience truth, channel performance, or legal approval.
