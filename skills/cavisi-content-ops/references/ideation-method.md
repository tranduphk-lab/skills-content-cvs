# Ideation Method

## Content matrix

Generate ideas from:

`Audience x Tension x Awareness Level x Pillar x Angle x Format x Channel x CTA`

## Workflow

1. Select a specific audience tension.
2. Select an awareness level.
3. Choose a pillar that can legitimately answer the tension.
4. Choose one angle:
   - question-first;
   - role clarity;
   - myth versus mechanism;
   - everyday observation;
   - source/status check;
   - routine decision;
   - product-system reveal.
5. Define one proofable promise.
6. Generate hook directions using the hook engine.
7. Choose a format and CTA.
8. Mark evidence and review requirements.
9. Score and deduplicate.

## Scoring

Score 1-5:

- audience relevance;
- brand fit;
- clarity;
- novelty within the current plan;
- proofability;
- channel fit;
- production feasibility.

Reject or revise any idea with proofability or brand fit below 4.

## Deduplication

Two ideas are duplicates when they share the same audience tension, primary promise, and payoff even if their titles or hooks differ.

## Idea status

An idea is not approved copy. Label it `IDEA` until evidence and claim checks are complete.
