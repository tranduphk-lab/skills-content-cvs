# Planning Method

## Planning levels

- Annual/quarterly: objectives, pillar balance, campaigns, capacity.
- Monthly: themes, topic mix, channel allocation, production cadence.
- Weekly: briefs, owners, review states, publish dates, repurpose actions.

## Required planning fields

Every scheduled unit needs:

- content ID;
- objective and funnel stage;
- audience and tension;
- pillar, angle, awareness level;
- primary promise and CTA;
- format and primary channel;
- owner role and approver role;
- source/review status;
- production and publishing states;
- repurpose destinations;
- test hypothesis when relevant.

## Capacity rules

- Treat available production capacity as a hard constraint.
- Do not invent budget, people, or turnaround time.
- Use role placeholders when names are absent.
- Reserve capacity for review-heavy evidence content.
- Do not schedule dependent content before required product/claim records are available.

## Balance rules

Avoid plans where:

- every post sells the product;
- every post is abstract brand philosophy;
- the same primary promise is repeated under different titles;
- all channels receive identical assets;
- Level 5 vision content dominates before the audience understands the category and system.
