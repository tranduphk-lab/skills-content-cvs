# Cavisi Brand DNA Snapshot

**Snapshot date:** 24 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Portable brand memory for `cavisi-content-ops`.  
**Authority:** This snapshot is a working synthesis, not a replacement for newer approved product, legal, science, asset, or channel records.

## 1. Brand Core

- **Name:** Cavisi
- **Category:** Scalp-care cosmetics organized through a Scalp-first philosophy.
- **One-line description:** A scalp-care brand that connects Shampoo and Spray in a clear two-step care system.
- **Brand heart:** `Hiểu đúng để chăm sóc đúng từ da đầu.`
- **Internal essence:** `Scalp-first clarity / Rõ từ gốc.`
- **Communication promise:** Make role, source, and limitation clear so people can make informed choices.
- **Stage:** Pre-launch for website production; commercial stage, market footprint, and official domain are not fully documented.
- **Official tagline:** Not locked in the current package. Proposed lines must not be presented as approved.

## 2. Positioning

Cavisi is for people who need a clearer, more maintainable way to care for the scalp after trying multiple options or wanting to care proactively. Cavisi starts from the scalp rather than only the visible hair surface and organizes the experience through two complementary roles:

- **Shampoo:** the cleansing foundation.
- **Spray:** the continuing-care step.

The intended territory is not a treatment promise. It is a clearer, source-aware scalp-care system that explains what each step is for and how certain the information is.

### Differentiation

- Scalp-first rather than hair-surface-only framing.
- Two-step role clarity rather than two disconnected SKUs.
- Science with boundaries rather than scientific-looking persuasion.
- Informed choice rather than pressure or exaggerated promises.

### Cavisi is not

- A medicine, diagnosis, or substitute for medical advice.
- A `combo trị gàu` promise.
- A miracle transformation or instant-result brand.
- A clinical authority unless claim-specific approval exists.
- A brand that uses microbiome language as proof by itself.

## 3. Audience

### Primary audience

People with recurring dandruff or oil concerns who have tried multiple products and are cautious about advertising, claim strength, and unclear instructions.

### Secondary audience

People who proactively care about the scalp and are willing to learn about microbiome-related concepts when explained plainly and responsibly.

### Jobs to be done

- Understand what the scalp-care problem or question actually is.
- Know what role each product has in a routine.
- Decide what information is trustworthy.
- Build a routine that is understandable and maintainable.
- Avoid being pushed by fear, hype, or scientific vocabulary without context.

### Common questions

- `Khác dầu gội trị gàu thông thường ở đâu?`
- `Spray có cần thiết không?`
- `Bao lâu dùng?`
- `Có an toàn không?`
- `Postbiotic là gì?`
- `Dữ liệu ở đâu?`
- `Đây là mỹ phẩm hay thuốc?`

### What the audience should be able to say

- Cavisi is a scalp-care brand, not a medicine.
- Shampoo and Spray have different but connected roles.
- Microbiome Care is a direction or framework, not automatic proof of a finished-product result.
- Cavisi distinguishes what is known, what is proposed, and what is not yet public.

## 4. Personality and Voice

### Archetype

- **Primary:** Sage, approximately 70%.
- **Supporting:** Caregiver, approximately 30%.

### Voice qualities

- Scientific but understandable.
- Transparent by status.
- Precise about certainty.
- Calm and balanced.
- Useful rather than persuasive.
- Warm without being childish or overfamiliar.
- Respectful without speaking for the audience.

### Preferred language

Use when accurate:

- `Scalp-first`
- `hệ chăm sóc hai bước`
- `làm sạch nền`
- `chăm sóc tiếp nối`
- `rõ vai trò`
- `rõ nguồn`
- `rõ giới hạn`
- `đã đối chiếu`
- `chưa công bố`
- `được thiết kế`
- `giúp` or `hỗ trợ` only when the source supports that verb.

### Avoid

- `trị`, `chữa`, `diệt`, `khỏi`, `tận gốc`, `cam kết`, `100%`, `ngay lập tức` without claim-specific approval.
- Shame about dandruff, oil, appearance, or social rejection.
- Scientific terms used only to sound authoritative.
- Borrowed medical, clinical, dermatologist, or celebrity authority.
- Fake urgency, scarcity, countdowns, or pressure to buy.
- Speaking as if Cavisi knows exactly what every user feels.

## 5. Message Hierarchy

### Core message

Cavisi helps people understand and maintain scalp care through a two-step system with clear roles, sources, and limitations.

### Supporting messages

1. Start with the scalp.
2. Shampoo and Spray are connected but do different jobs.
3. Science should clarify, not perform authority.
4. Sources and limitations are part of care.
5. A useful routine should be understandable and maintainable.

### Message translation rule

Every content unit should clarify at least one of:

- what is being cared for;
- what role a step has;
- what the information is based on;
- what the information does not establish.

## 6. Product Architecture

```text
Cavisi
└── Two-step scalp-care system
    ├── Cavisi Shampoo — cleansing foundation
    └── Cavisi Spray — continuing-care step
```

When discussing one product, preserve the system context when useful. Do not make the pair sound like unrelated products or imply that every person must buy both.

## 7. Content DNA

### Default pillars

1. **Hiểu da đầu:** foundational education and audience language.
2. **Rõ từng bước:** role clarity for Shampoo, Spray, and the system.
3. **Evidence có giới hạn:** source, status, claim literacy, and what evidence does not prove.
4. **Routine dễ duy trì:** decision support and practical organization.
5. **Cavisi world:** philosophy, responsible care, people, and community when real source material exists.

### Default content spine

`Hook rõ → tension hợp lệ → Scalp-first reframe → cơ chế/vai trò → nguồn → giới hạn → payoff → một CTA`

### Practical retention mechanisms

- Use a clear question, conflict, or decision tension early.
- Convert everyday routine observations into insight through relationship, tradeoff, empathy, and conflict.
- Match depth to awareness: recognition → reduced effort → avoid misunderstanding → solve → informed belief.
- Use curiosity without hiding the topic or inflating the claim.

## 8. Visual and Story Boundaries

- Visuals may clarify a role, sequence, comparison, or source status.
- Do not use mascot, linh vật, or a recurring brand character in Cavisi content or visual direction.
- Human subjects, if used, must be documentary or contextual; they must not act as a mascot, fictional spokesperson, testimonial, or proof.
- Keep evidence and disclaimers readable; do not subordinate them to decorative marketing.
- Product visuals must use approved artwork and asset status.

## 9. Claim and Evidence Status

### Status vocabulary

- `brand-approved`: permitted at brand-strategy level.
- `product-pending`: needs product/legal confirmation.
- `science-pending`: needs scientific review; do not infer a finished-product result.
- `publication-pending`: source/publication permission is unresolved.
- `not-public`: do not imply public availability.
- `prohibited-until-approved`: do not use in production.

### Safe working records

- `CAV-BRAND-001`: Cavisi is a scalp-care brand organized through Scalp-first philosophy — brand-level only.
- `CAV-SYSTEM-001`: Shampoo is cleansing foundation; Spray is continuing-care step — final wording must match approved records.
- `CAV-TRUST-001`: Cavisi aims to clarify role, source, and limitation — do not claim full implementation unless verified.
- `CAV-MICRO-001`: Microbiome Care is a brand direction — not finished-product efficacy proof.

### High-risk until approved

- 24-hour efficacy.
- Barrier repair.
- Microbiome restoration or balance as a finished-product result.
- Clinical or dermatologist-tested claims.
- Leave-on, no-rinse, daily frequency, or other directions not confirmed by product records.
- Treatment, cure, diagnosis, root-cause, guaranteed, or universal-result language.

## 10. Decision Filter

Before approving a content direction, ask:

1. Does it begin from the scalp perspective?
2. Does it clarify a meaningful role, question, or choice?
3. Does the certainty of the wording match the source?
4. Can the audience find the source or limitation when needed?
5. Does it sound Sage/Caregiver: capable, calm, useful, and non-coercive?
6. Does it avoid fear, shame, borrowed authority, and pressure?
7. Is the primary promise and CTA singular?

## 15. Plain-language definition of Scalp-first

On first public use, write Scalp-first — bắt đầu từ da đầu. Explain it as a way to organize care around understanding the scalp and the role of each step, rather than looking only at visible hair. It is not a treatment or efficacy promise.
